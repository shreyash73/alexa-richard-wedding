The Celebrations

Wedding Festivities

✦
Haldi11th November · Morning

A joyful morning ceremony where turmeric is lovingly applied to the bride and groom — blessings, laughter, and marigold all around.

Sangeet11th November · Evening

An evening of music, dance, and celebration as both families come together to sing, perform, and dance the night away.

Wedding12th November · Morning

The sacred pheras and exchange of vows around the holy fire as Harshwardhan & Bhumika begin their journey together.

Reception12th November · Evening

A grand evening of feasting, toasts, and revelry to celebrate the newlyweds with all our beloved family and friends.

The Aesthetic

Dress Code

✦

We would be delighted to see you dressed in festive Indian attire. Let these warm, vibrant tones inspire your outfits across the celebrations:

Marigold
Terracotta
Deep Maroon
Antique Gold
Sage

For Ladies

Sarees, lehengas, or anarkalis in bright festive hues. Bright florals for the Haldi, and your finest silks and jewellery for the Wedding & Reception.

For Gentlemen

Kurtas and sherwanis in warm tones. Keep it light and colourful for the daytime functions, and go regal for the evening celebrations.
